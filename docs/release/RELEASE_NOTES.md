# Release notes

## 0.1.0 — limited beta (2026-11-25)

The first beta of Resolve: the dashboard and the browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Brave.

**Accounts and groups**

- Create an account, set a display name, create groups, invite people with single- or multi-use
  links, promote and remove members, rename or dissolve a group, and delete your account.

**Website protection**

- Admins keep a confidential list of non-negotiable and additional websites; members can add
  websites without approval but cannot see the list. Essential services always stay reachable.
- The extension blocks the active group's websites, keeps working offline with the last rules, and
  shows when protection is on, starting, stale or failing.

**On-device media blurring**

- Optional: images and videos are blurred until an AI model running in your browser checks them.
  Nothing is uploaded. Three sensitivity levels; media that cannot be checked stays blurred by
  default.

**Voluntary accountability**

- Each member chooses who may receive their alerts (Private by default, selected members or
  everyone); recipients decide whether to receive them. Admins cannot change either.
- Two alerts only: a deliberate visit to a non-negotiable website (never naming the website), and a
  member leaving or being removed. Emails are generic and say an alert is not proof of intent.

**Install:** the beta is installed from a zip (Load unpacked); see BETA_TESTERS.md. Store listings
follow.

**Known limitations**

- Firefox is not supported yet.
- The extension can be turned off or removed; other browsers and devices are not covered.
- The media filter makes mistakes in both directions; its miss rate has not been measured yet.
- Not every blocked visit produces an alert (for example ones a page or ad caused).
