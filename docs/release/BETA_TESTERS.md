# Resolve beta: how to install

Thank you for testing Resolve. This beta runs on Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Brave on a
computer (not phones). Firefox is not supported yet.

## 1. Create your account

Open **https://main.dcwo6nhqxltjm.amplifyapp.com**, choose **Sign in**, then **Create an
account**. Add a display name on your profile so your group recognises you. If someone invited
you, open their invitation link after signing in.

## 2. Install the extension

Until Resolve is in the browser stores, it is installed from a file:

1. Download `resolve-0.1.0-beta-chrome.zip` from the link you were sent and **unzip** it to a
   folder you will keep (the browser loads it from there; do not delete the folder).
2. Open the extensions page: `chrome://extensions` (Chrome, Brave) or `edge://extensions` (Edge).
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right in Chrome, left side in Edge).
4. Choose **Load unpacked** and select the unzipped folder (the one containing `manifest.json`).
5. Pin Resolve: click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to Resolve.

The browser may show a notice about developer-mode extensions; that is expected for a beta
installed this way.

## 3. Turn on protection

1. Click the Resolve icon and **Sign in** with the same account.
2. Choose your **Active group**. The popup shows "Domain protection: on" once your group's
   blocked websites apply.
3. Optional, in **Settings**: turn on **Blur sensitive images and videos**, and if you share
   accountability alerts, **Alert my accountability partners about blocked visits**. The browser
   asks for permission for each; Resolve explains what each permission is used for.

Then follow **Getting started** on the dashboard for groups, sharing and consent.

## Good to know

- Resolve is a supporting tool and can be turned off or removed; it is not a guarantee.
- Images are checked on your device and never uploaded. Alerts never say which website.
- Updates: when a new beta zip is sent, replace the folder's contents and press the reload
  button on Resolve in the extensions page.

## Feedback

Use **Support** on the dashboard. Please never send websites you visited or screenshots of pages;
describe what you did and what you expected instead.
